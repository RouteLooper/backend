Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the RouteLooper mobile application is handled by Arpan Studios.

Information We Access

The app uses your device’s location to generate looped routes for running, cycling, or driving.

Location Data

Your location is used only to calculate routes. Location data is processed on your device and is not stored, collected, or transmitted by the developer.

Third-Party Services

This app uses the Google Maps SDK to display maps and open routes. When using the map functionality, certain data may be transmitted to Google in order to provide mapping services.

Google may collect information such as device information, usage data, and approximate location as described in its own privacy policy.

For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Collection

The app does not collect personal information, does not require user accounts, and does not store user data.

Data Sharing

Aside from information processed by Google to provide map services, the developer does not share personal data with third parties.

Permissions

  • Location: Used to generate routes starting from your current position.
  • Internet: Used to retrieve map and routing data.

Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
